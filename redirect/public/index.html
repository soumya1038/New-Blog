Lekhon is a writing, publishing, and social storytelling platform where people create blogs, articles, short posts, stories, profiles, and community conversations.

Lekhon uses Google Sign-In only for authentication, account creation, account access, and profile setup. With user consent, Lekhon may receive basic Google account information such as name, email address, and profile image to help users access and manage their Lekhon account.
